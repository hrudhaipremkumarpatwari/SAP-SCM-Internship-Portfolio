# Six-Assessment Index

This file provides a recruiter-friendly index of the complete SAP SCM internship assessment series.

| Week | Project | Business question | Main SAP SCM concepts | Primary evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Chain Network Planning | How should the supply network be structured to balance service, capacity, inventory and transport? | SNP, locations, products, sourcing, transportation lanes, planning versions, deployment and TLB concepts | Final report, network diagrams, configuration checklist, risk/validation plan |
| 2 | Master Data & Inventory Optimization | How can reliable planning data and inventory parameters reduce stock-out and excess-inventory risk? | Product/location master data, CIF concepts, safety stock, lead time, lot size, EOQ, ABC-XYZ | Final report, data mapping, inventory analysis, risk/validation plan |
| 3 | Logistics & Distribution Network Design | How can inventory transfers and transportation decisions improve utilization, cost and service? | Distribution planning, transportation lanes, inventory transfer, route planning, shipment consolidation, TM concepts | Final report, route analysis, distribution planning, diagrams |
| 4 | Production Planning & Scheduling Analysis | How can weekly demand be converted into a feasible capacity-aware production schedule? | PP/DS, resources, calendars, finite capacity, changeovers, bottlenecks, recovery planning | Final report, worked calculations, production schedule, recovery flow |
| 5 | Supply Chain Integration & Collaboration | How should a supplier delay propagate through planning, production, inventory, logistics and customer-facing processes? | ERP/SCM integration, CIF context, S/4HANA PP/DS context, event propagation, reconciliation, exception ownership | Final report, event timeline, integration architecture, validation tests |
| 6 | Performance Evaluation & Continuous Improvement | How can KPI trends be converted into controlled improvement actions? | KPI definitions, reporting concepts, trend analysis, validation, PDCA, improvement roadmap | Final report, simulated KPI dataset, KPI analysis, 30/60/90-day roadmap |

## Core Business Scenario

Most assessments use the same fictional learning scenario, **Deccan Harvest Foods Pvt. Ltd.**, a millet-based ready-to-cook food manufacturer serving markets in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka. Reusing the scenario allows the assessments to connect instead of appearing as unrelated exercises.

## Evidence Pattern

Across the series, the assessment method is intentionally consistent:

**Business scenario → assumptions/data → SAP functional concept → worked analysis → issue/constraint → corrective action → KPI/validation → risk/control**

This structure is intended to demonstrate practical functional reasoning rather than only listing SAP terminology.
