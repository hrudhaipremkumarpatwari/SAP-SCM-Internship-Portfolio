> **Separate training example:** This workbook and its CSV exports are synthetic, independently modeled learning data. They are not part of the six Deccan Harvest assessment cases linked from the portfolio hub and must not be treated as a reconciliation or extension of those cases. ## Scenario at a glance

The modeled case uses one synthetic finished product (FG-01), one component (RM-01), a two-component-per-finished-unit assumption, INR, and six monthly buckets from January through June 2026. The before/after scenario changes forecast review, supplier choice, production capacity, and distribution design together. Reported differences are modeled outcomes, not measured benefits or causal estimates.

Use this example on its own. The linked assessment repositories retain their own products, time periods, units, and case assumptions; do not combine the datasets without reconciling them first.
